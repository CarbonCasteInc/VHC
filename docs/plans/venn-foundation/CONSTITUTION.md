# Venn Constitution

> Status: Pre-Ratification Draft 0.1
> Founding Custodian: Carbon Caste Inc
> Initial Legal Context: Canada, with Ontario as the operating base
> Companion Drafts: `FOUNDING_DOCUMENT.md`, `CHARTER.md`, `ROADMAP.md`

## Preamble

Venn is constituted so that collective human judgment may remain legible and consequential in the age of artificial intelligence without making individual people legible to power.

This Constitution binds the Venn steward, Carbon Caste as founding custodian, official operators, reference implementations, conformance mark, and any artifact or measure represented as conformant Venn output. It governs what Venn admits, records, transforms, publishes, and claims. It cannot control a hostile fork or every act of an external platform; it therefore pairs technical admissibility with licensing, mark, audit, contractual, and public-accountability mechanisms and states their limits honestly.

## Article I — Authority and interpretation

### 1. Normative language

`MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `REQUIRED`, `SHALL`, and `SHALL NOT` are binding. `SHOULD` identifies a presumptive requirement that may be departed from only with documented justification. `MAY` grants permission.

### 2. Order of authority

For conformant Venn activity, authority descends in this order:

1. the Foundational Covenant in Article XV;
2. protected provisions of this Constitution;
3. the Charter;
4. ordinary constitutional provisions;
5. ratified specifications and registries;
6. conformance tests;
7. operating policy; and
8. reference implementation behavior.

Lower authority cannot silently override higher authority. A conflict is resolved upward or treated as a defect.

### 3. Interpretation

Ambiguity SHALL be resolved toward:

- human control over confirmation and action;
- evidence-class separation;
- data minimization and unlinkability;
- disclosed uncertainty and limits;
- provider and implementer plurality;
- protection of fundamental rights;
- practical auditability; and
- the least authority necessary for the stated purpose.

## Article II — Purpose, scope, and claims

### 1. Present purpose

Venn presently measures and communicates human signal for defined populations. It does not derive political authority merely from measurement.

### 2. Long-term purpose

Venn SHALL be designed so that communities may eventually choose separately governed Venn decision or ballot profiles for binding collective decisions. Such use requires voluntary public adoption, legal authorization where applicable, independent administration and implementation, and satisfaction of Article XIV. It is not inherited from the opinion protocol.

### 3. Domain progression

The first person-linked signal profile is adult political and civic measurement in a bounded Ontario population. Commercial, cultural, organizational, and other preference domains MAY follow only through named profiles with domain-appropriate consent, publication, calibration, and use restrictions.

### 4. Honest scope

Every measure SHALL identify its claim, version, population, jurisdiction, eligibility policy, period, evidence classes, assurance policy, deduplication scope, calibration method, and limitations.

No current participant population may be described as humanity. No majority measurement may be treated as authority to eliminate fundamental rights.

## Article III — Signal semantics

### 1. Required classes

Conformant records and measures SHALL distinguish at least:

- `expression`: an interaction occurred under a declared origin and integrity basis;
- `salience`: a subject appears important within a declared participant set;
- `inferred`: a registered model assigns probabilities to possible meanings;
- `confirmed`: a human explicitly authorizes an exact claim-bound position;
- `delegated_action`: a human authorizes a bounded external act; and
- `ballot`: a separately governed voting event satisfying Article XIV.

Calibration is an output method, not an input evidence class. Contributor assurance is orthogonal to signal meaning.

### 2. Expression and inference

Expression MAY inform salience, intensity, frame discovery, question discovery, and clearly labelled probabilistic aggregate evidence.

Expression or inference MUST NOT:

- be represented as the person's assertion;
- create a confirmed position without a fresh claim-bound human act;
- create a ballot, delegation, petition signature, or message to an institution;
- enter a person-counted measure without an authorized measurement pathway; or
- be exposed as an identity-linked political profile by a Venn role.

A model MAY suggest a possible position to the participant. It MUST NOT select, pre-confirm, or submit the position. Silence and inaction are never assent.

### 3. Repetition

Repeated expression MAY affect bounded frequency, salience, intensity, or inference confidence under a published saturation function. It MUST NOT multiply distinct-human headcount.

Published measures SHALL keep distinct-human reach, expression frequency, intensity, interpretation confidence, and representativeness separate.

### 4. Confirmation

A confirmed position requires a fresh human-facing ceremony displaying the exact claim, version, selected outcome, and consequence. Compose-and-Confirm SHALL offer an equally accessible path to express without being counted.

No implementation may claim that device authentication alone proves comprehension, freedom from coercion, unique personhood, residence, or formal voting intent.

## Article IV — Layered authorization

### 1. Separate permissions

Venn SHALL maintain distinct, independently revocable permissions for:

1. expression measurement;
2. claim confirmation;
3. eligibility and deduplication;
4. calibration research;
5. delegated action;
6. voting; and
7. secondary use, including model training.

Permission for one MUST NOT imply permission for another.

### 2. Valid authorization

Person-linked political-expression analysis requires authorization that is:

- specific and purpose-bound;
- informed in plain language;
- affirmative rather than inferred;
- freely given without unrelated detriment;
- granular where purposes or recipients differ;
- demonstrable without publishing political participation;
- reviewable by the participant;
- renewable at an appropriate interval; and
- as easy to withdraw as to grant.

Authorization cannot legitimize a prohibited, disproportionate, discriminatory, or otherwise unlawful purpose.

### 3. Standing measurement authorization

An enrolled participant MAY grant standing expression-measurement authorization. It SHALL be limited by surface or source, purpose, evidence use, recipient class, retention, and time. The participant SHALL be able to inspect and withdraw it.

Host, publisher, or platform permission does not replace participant permission. A platform MUST NOT volunteer its users' political histories to Venn.

### 4. Public expression

Public expression MAY inform event, claim, question, and frame discovery where the source-rights and legal basis permit. Without an authorized measurement pathway, it MUST NOT enter a person-counted public-opinion measure or produce a durable person-linked inference.

### 5. Secondary use

Model training, targeted messaging, advertising, sale, disclosure to a new recipient, and materially new research purposes require separate authority. Training on identifiable or linkable political expression is prohibited by default and cannot be enabled through general service terms.

### 6. Adults first

The initial political-expression inference, confirmed-position aggregation, location-bound political credential, calibration-panel, and delegated-political-action profiles are restricted to people aged 18 or older.

A youth profile requires independent child-development, education, ethics, rights, legal, security, and privacy review; age-appropriate explanation; jurisdiction-specific capacity rules; protection against coercion; stronger publication thresholds; no targeted advertising or content; and no durable political profile. Parental authorization alone is insufficient.

## Article V — Identity, personhood, and human count

### 1. Separation of functions

Account control, authenticator control, personhood, eligibility, location, deduplication, confirmation, and ballot authority are separate claims. No credential is treated as proving properties it does not establish.

### 2. Governing principle

Venn requires neither a mandatory globally linkable identifier nor dependence on any single identity provider. Widely adopted identity or personhood credentials may contribute to assurance only through scope-specific, privacy-preserving proofs and provider-neutral alternatives.

Venn SHALL NOT collect raw biometrics, operate biometric-enrollment hardware, issue a global Venn identifier, or maintain a global uniqueness index.

### 3. Scoped count

Venn may claim a distinct eligible participant only within a declared measurement scope and credential-admission policy that support that conclusion.

The scope SHALL bind at least:

`{claim, claim-version, population, period, credential-admission-policy}`.

Less-assured or unresolved cohorts SHALL remain separately labelled. Provider-local counts MUST NOT be added as globally distinct humans unless a reviewed cross-provider construction supports that operation.

### 4. Long-term assurance

Venn SHALL pursue stronger one-human/one-count evidence through interoperable providers, scope-bound nullifiers, same-holder proofs, cross-provider overlap research, independent audits, recovery and revocation, published limits, and progressively stronger personhood evidence.

Provider popularity alone never makes a credential mandatory. False-duplicate, exclusion, outage, accessibility, recovery, revocation, and issuer-capture risks SHALL be disclosed and appealable.

### 5. Passkeys and device biometrics

Passkeys authorize control of a relying-party-scoped credential. Local biometrics or a device PIN may unlock that credential but are not visible to Venn and do not prove a unique natural person. Conformant claims SHALL preserve this distinction.

## Article VI — Privacy and custody

### 1. Three planes

Venn SHALL separate:

1. a private participant and source plane;
2. a purpose-bound, role-separated processing and audit plane; and
3. a public aggregate and transparency plane.

Readable per-person expression, inference, position, account linkage, scope-nullifier mapping, receipt, or ballot SHALL NOT enter the public plane.

### 2. Prohibited dossier

No Venn measurement, aggregation, log, conformance, or publication role may hold both:

- a stable cross-context person identifier; and
- readable expression, inference, position, or ballot data.

The participant MAY maintain an encrypted private history for their own use. An originating platform may retain raw content it already lawfully controls, but its Venn adapter MUST NOT create a new durable account-keyed political-inference dossier.

### 3. Public stance ledger prohibition

Venn SHALL NOT publish per-person stance rows, nullifiers, participation proofs, fine-grained timestamps, or per-submission commitments capable of reconstructing political history or creating a participation oracle.

### 4. Right not to be measured

No conformant public query may confirm or deny a person's participation. Refusal, withdrawal, abstention, and non-participation SHALL not be made distinguishable to an unauthorized third party.

Withdrawal effects, deletion limits, already-published aggregate treatment, correction paths, and legally required retention SHALL be disclosed before authorization.

### 5. Aggregation and release

Deduplication SHALL be separated from readable signal. Any profile claiming non-collusion SHALL use independently governed aggregation roles and disclose their operators and trust assumptions.

Every output class SHALL have a registered threshold, suppression rule, contribution bound, query discipline, and privacy accounting. Small cells, repeated releases, metadata, status checks, timing, and differencing remain in the threat model.

### 6. Receipts and coercion

Confirmed-position and ballot designs SHALL minimize transferable proof and participation oracles. No live confirmed-position profile may claim receipt-freeness, deniability, coercion resistance, or outcome privacy until its exact construction and metadata behavior pass independent cryptographic and coercion review.

## Article VII — Reference artifacts and claim governance

Every conformant reference artifact SHALL include versioned claims, evidence, contested facts, uncertainty, frame and counterframe, source-rights evidence, wording history, and corrections.

Claims SHALL have stable identifiers and exact versions. Material wording changes create new versions. Paraphrase sensitivity, translation, and framing risk SHALL be measured where consequential.

Multiple independent publishers may create competing conformant artifacts. Venn SHALL maintain appeals, correction, provenance, and semantic-mapping processes but SHALL NOT grant one publisher permanent canonical authority.

## Article VIII — Measures, calibration, and publication

### 1. Required disclosure

Every public measure SHALL disclose:

- result and response schema;
- claim and version;
- population, jurisdiction, eligibility, and denominator source;
- measurement period;
- evidence-class and assurance composition;
- contributor counts and deduplication limits;
- coverage, acquisition surfaces, and known skew;
- calibration and nonresponse method;
- uncertainty;
- model and methodology versions;
- privacy protections and release accounting;
- wording stability;
- correction and supersession history; and
- a machine-readable envelope.

### 2. Representativeness

Participation volume, credential assurance, and selection integrity do not establish representativeness.

Venn MAY claim representativeness only when sampling, frame coverage, nonresponse treatment, weighting, calibration, and uncertainty justify the claim. Otherwise it SHALL describe a participant observation or a qualified modelled estimate.

### 3. Public civic baseline

Any political or civic measure published under the Venn name SHALL have a freely accessible public baseline containing the disclosures in this Article.

No paying recipient may receive exclusive access to the underlying civic result or a materially earlier opportunity to act on secretly measured public opinion.

### 4. Private profiles

Commercial and cultural research MAY generate private outputs only under a separately named profile that identifies recipients to participants. It MUST NOT be represented as an open Venn civic measure or evade the prohibitions on personal profiling, targeting, discrimination, and secondary use.

## Article IX — Agents, platforms, and products

Agents MAY resolve artifacts, claims, frames, evidence, methodology, measures, and corrections; verify envelopes; preserve a participant's private preferences; and request human confirmation.

No general agent interface SHALL expose `record_stance` or an equivalent operation that allows an agent to create a confirmed position without a trusted human surface.

Delegations SHALL be scoped, expiring, revocable, attenuating, and budget-bound. High-impact action requires fresh human authorization.

Platforms MAY host adapters and aggregation roles, but participant authorization, evidence semantics, role separation, and public disclosure remain required. Venn-specific sensitive outputs MUST NOT be used for individual political targeting, discriminatory moderation, eligibility discrimination, or covert manipulation.

Venn Reader is a first-party reference product, not the protocol. It receives no private specification, privileged data, weighting, conformance, or governance authority.

## Article X — Commerce, openness, and conformance

Core specifications, registries, conformance suites, and test vectors SHALL be royalty-free and practically independently implementable. Exact licences remain subject to ratification and legal review.

Venn operators MAY charge for hosting, timely public APIs, exploration tools, dashboards, integration, artifact production, calibration, auditing, support, and availability.

Venn operators MUST NOT sell:

- individual political profiles or readable per-person inference;
- raw cross-platform political histories;
- political targeting segments;
- exclusive civic results;
- preferential protocol influence; or
- identity-linked ballot or signal history.

Open code alone does not establish neutrality. The Venn mark and official network SHALL use conformance, audit, appeal, suspension, and revocation procedures against false assurance claims, surveillance uses, semantic drift, and rights violations while preserving the right to fork under a different identity.

## Article XI — Custody and governance

### 1. Founding custody

Carbon Caste Inc is the disclosed founding custodian and commercial reference operator. During formation it MAY administer repositories, domains, infrastructure, and the mark; fund development; employ contributors; operate Venn Reader; and integrate reviewed changes.

Carbon Caste:

- has no permanent constitutional veto or permanent governing seat;
- MUST NOT sell exclusive protocol control;
- SHALL account separately for protocol governance and commercial operation;
- SHALL disclose material funding and conflicts;
- SHALL give conforming implementations equal technical standing;
- SHALL preserve independent implementability; and
- SHALL transfer or irrevocably license governance rights and the mark to an independent steward.

### 2. Transition gate

An independent stewardship body SHALL exist before:

- general public collection of person-linked political signals; or
- public conformance is granted to an implementation not operated by Carbon Caste.

Bounded, counsel-approved adult pilots MAY precede the transition when their purpose, population, processors, risks, and non-general status are public.

Exact legal form and transfer mechanics require legal and participant deliberation. They cannot negate the transition obligation or Foundational Covenant.

### 3. Mature governance

The independent steward SHALL provide fixed terms, disclosed funding and conflicts, recorded decisions, public RFCs, appeals, expiring emergency authority, independent mark control, and meaningful participation by users, implementers, operators, auditors, civil-rights, privacy, accessibility, methodology, publisher, institutional, geographic, and jurisdictional constituencies.

No founder, company, sponsor, platform, government, token holder, or credential provider may control the protocol.

### 4. Progressive self-government

During formation, Venn measurements of founder, reviewer, partner, and prospective-user views are advisory. Carbon Caste decides and publishes its reasoning.

Ordinary product and registry decisions MAY become binding on verified participants when the rule, population, options, threshold, and consequences are declared in advance.

Constitutional governance SHALL later use Venn's reference, signal, deliberation, and calibration machinery with Article XV safeguards. Each process SHALL state whom it represents. A self-selected early user population MUST NOT be described as humanity.

## Article XII — Security and cryptographic evolution

Security claims SHALL bind an exact protocol version, implementation, threat model, operator set, and evidence packet.

Venn SHALL maintain:

- a cryptographic dependency and data-longevity inventory;
- versioned algorithm suites and negotiation;
- deprecation, compromise, rotation, and migration procedures;
- interoperability fixtures for transitions;
- independent cryptographic, privacy, coercion, accessibility, and operational review;
- hybrid classical/post-quantum trials where justified; and
- post-quantum planning for information requiring long-lived confidentiality or future ballot validity.

“State of the art,” “quantum-safe,” “trustless,” “anonymous,” and “privacy-preserving” MUST NOT be used without naming the mechanism, assumptions, review state, and residual risks.

## Article XIII — Delegated action

A confirmed position does not authorize delivery or action. Contacting an institution, signing a petition, spending a delegation budget, or directing a familiar requires fresh, specific, revocable authorization.

Every action receipt SHALL identify the authority, scope, expiry, consequence, destination, and revocation state without exposing unrelated signal history.

Agents MUST NOT broaden authority by inference. Delegation attenuates; it does not self-expand.

## Article XIV — Ballot boundary

Venn aspires to become sufficiently trustworthy, verifiable, and publicly legitimate that communities may freely choose it for binding democratic decisions.

No ordinary expression, inference, confirmed position, delegation, or public-opinion estimate constitutes a ballot.

A ballot profile requires:

- fresh voter authorization;
- eligibility without linkage to signal history;
- ballot secrecy;
- receipt-freeness and coercion resistance;
- universal verifiability;
- accessibility;
- availability and denial-of-service resilience;
- contestability, recount, and correction procedures;
- independent implementations and administration;
- legal certification where applicable;
- cryptographic agility and post-quantum migration planning; and
- separation from Venn's commercial operator.

Permitted progression is:

1. nonbinding internal consultation;
2. binding ordinary Venn community decisions;
3. low-stakes organizational votes;
4. independently administered civic pilots; and
5. legally recognized public decisions only after external validation and adoption.

Every deployment is separately gated. Brand trust cannot substitute for verification.

## Article XV — Entrenchment and amendment

### 1. Foundational Covenant

The following define Venn's constitutional identity:

1. Humans control confirmation of their beliefs.
2. Inference never silently becomes a position.
3. No public personal stance ledger is permitted.
4. No mandatory globally linkable identity is permitted.
5. No single-provider dependency is permitted.
6. Signal history never silently becomes a ballot.
7. Fundamental rights are not subject to measured popularity.
8. The protocol remains independently implementable.
9. Public measures disclose their limitations.

Changing a Foundational Covenant creates a successor constitutional order. It requires explicit refounding, a new protocol version identity, full public notice, preserved prior artifacts, and a protected right to remain on or fork the preceding version. It is not an amendment to the same Venn order.

### 2. Protected provisions

Protected provisions include layered consent, adult-first initial political participation, provider neutrality, privacy placement, public civic baselines, Carbon Caste's transition obligation, independent stewardship, the ballot boundary, rights protections, and amendment safeguards.

A protected amendment requires:

- a published proposal with exact versioned wording;
- independent legal, technical, security, privacy, accessibility, methodology, and rights review as applicable;
- a public deliberation period;
- two temporally separated approvals;
- defined approval across users, implementers, and the steward;
- an impact and migration assessment;
- full re-ratification of the resulting document; and
- the preserved right to remain on or fork the preceding version.

Exact thresholds remain to be ratified. No interested constituency may unilaterally approve its own expansion of power.

### 3. Ordinary amendment

Ordinary provisions and policy may use a simpler published RFC, review, decision, and versioning process. Ordinary amendment MUST NOT weaken a Foundational Covenant or protected provision by indirection.

### 4. Final ratification

Before initial ratification, all four foundational documents SHALL share one exact repository commit and pass the adopted constitutional review protocol. Model agreement is advisory. Final founding authority remains with the human founder acting transparently through Carbon Caste's disclosed formation process.

## Article XVI — Transitional provisions

1. The current documents are drafts and authorize no live political-signal collection.
2. No person-linked political signal SHALL be collected before Canadian privacy counsel reviews the data flows and a DPIA-equivalent assessment passes.
3. VHC is historical source material, not Venn's architectural foundation. Every port requires provenance, licensing, semantic, privacy, security, and conformance review.
4. VHC's defensible news, reference-artifact, story, and Compose-and-Confirm precursors MAY be ported into separate Venn reference or reader projects after hostile review.
5. Unresolved implementation questions SHALL remain explicit. A product deadline cannot silently decide constitutional meaning.
