# Venn Charter

> Status: Pre-Ratification Draft 0.1
> Founding Custodian: Carbon Caste Inc
> Initial Legal Context: Canada, with Ontario as the operating base
> Companion Drafts: `FOUNDING_DOCUMENT.md`, `CONSTITUTION.md`, `ROADMAP.md`

## 1. Purpose

Venn exists to create an open protocol through which human expression, judgment, and authorization can remain legible and consequential in an internet increasingly populated and operated by artificial agents.

Its public ambition is:

> **Venn is the open protocol for representing humanity in the age of AI. It turns everyday expression into evidence of what people care about, and deliberate human confirmation into trustworthy measures of what they believe. It helps platforms, agents, communities, and institutions count distinct humans rather than bots, posts, or noise — creating an auditable public-opinion layer without creating a public record of anyone's identity or political history.**

Its public tagline is:

> **Venn: humanity's public-opinion protocol for the age of AI.**

Its technical description is:

> **The human-signal protocol for the agentic internet.**

Venn begins as a measurement instrument. It may communicate confirmed positions, support purpose-limited delegation, and eventually become capable of supporting binding collective decisions when affected communities freely choose that use and independent evidence demonstrates that the system is superior to available alternatives. Measurement alone never gives Venn political authority.

## 2. Orienting statements

Every contributor, agent, implementer, operator, and steward shall use these statements to interpret ambiguity:

1. **Make collective human judgment legible to machines without making individual people legible to power.**
2. **Expression shows what matters. Only a human can confirm what they believe.**
3. **Count people once. Measure repetition as intensity, never as additional humans.**
4. **Public opinion should be public. Personal political histories should not.**
5. **Every signal remains what it is: attention is not belief, inference is not confirmation, and engagement is not a vote.**
6. **Humans confirm. Agents cite. Institutions verify. Nobody has to trust the operator.**
7. **No platform, identity provider, company, founder, or government should own humanity's signal.**

These are architectural instructions, not advertising copy.

## 3. What representing humanity means

Venn is built to support four progressively more consequential capabilities:

1. describe what defined populations appear to care about and believe;
2. communicate explicitly confirmed positions to institutions and publics;
3. let personal agents take explicitly authorized, revocable, purpose-limited action;
4. eventually support legitimate collective decisions when a community adopts a separately governed decision or ballot profile.

The fourth capability is a long-term desired outcome, not a present product claim. It must emerge through public legitimacy, voluntary adoption, legal authorization, independent implementation, and evidence that the system improves governance, representation, outcomes, and democratic ideals.

Venn never claims that a measured majority may abolish fundamental rights or that a participant sample speaks for humanity.

## 4. Scope

Venn begins with adult political, civic, and public-interest questions in a bounded Canadian implementation, initially operated from Ontario. Its protocol remains jurisdiction-neutral.

The long-term scope includes commercial, cultural, social, organizational, and other forms of human preference. Each domain requires a named measurement profile whose consent, privacy, publication, calibration, and governance rules match its risks.

Every measure represents only its declared:

- claim and version;
- population and eligibility policy;
- jurisdiction;
- period;
- evidence and assurance classes;
- deduplication scope;
- calibration method; and
- limitations.

Global ambition never licenses global overclaiming.

## 5. The instrument

Venn consists of three permanent layers:

### 5.1 Reference

Versioned, evidence-backed accounts of events and questions separate established facts, contested claims, uncertainty, frames, source rights, and corrections. Multiple independent publishers may produce conforming artifacts. Venn is not a ministry of truth.

### 5.2 Signal

The protocol keeps expression, salience, probabilistic inference, and human-confirmed position distinct. Contributor assurance is recorded separately from signal meaning.

Ordinary expression may reveal attention, intensity, framing, ambiguity, sarcasm, or probable position. It never silently becomes a person's confirmed belief.

### 5.3 Calibration

Probability samples, panels, surveys, and other defensible methods estimate participation gaps, coverage, bias, nonresponse, and uncertainty. Verification does not equal representativeness. Calibration is permanent infrastructure, not a temporary launch exercise.

## 6. Durable commitments

Venn commits to:

1. **Human authority.** Only a fresh human-facing authorization may create a confirmed position or consequential action.
2. **Typed evidence.** Expression, salience, inference, confirmation, delegation, and ballot are never collapsed into one score or silently converted into one another.
3. **Scoped human count.** Venn counts distinct eligible participants only within the credential, population, period, and nullifier scope supported by evidence. Less-assured signals remain separately labelled.
4. **Increasing assurance without universal tracking.** Venn pursues stronger one-human/one-count evidence without requiring a globally linkable identifier or one provider.
5. **Layered consent.** Permission for expression measurement, confirmation, eligibility, calibration, delegation, voting, or secondary use never implies permission for another.
6. **Data minimization.** Venn measures populations without constructing a centrally readable identity-linked political history.
7. **Public civic baseline.** Political and civic measures published under the Venn name have a freely accessible result, method, uncertainty, and machine-readable envelope.
8. **Epistemic honesty.** Every public measure exposes coverage, denominator, evidence composition, assurance, calibration, uncertainty, known skew, and correction history.
9. **Plural reference authority.** Claims, frames, translations, and corrections remain contestable and independently publishable.
10. **Provider neutrality.** No identity, platform, model, cloud, payment, publisher, or credential provider is constitutionally privileged.
11. **Independent implementation.** Specifications, registries, test vectors, and conformance requirements remain practically implementable without permission from Carbon Caste or a hosted Venn service.
12. **Replaceable trust.** Trust assumptions are explicit, bounded, auditable, and designed to become replaceable.
13. **Rights before popularity.** No measured majority may eliminate the privacy, dignity, accessibility, due-process, or participation rights of others.
14. **Security evolution.** Cryptography is versioned and migratable. Venn inventories dependencies, tests transitions, and prepares for post-quantum risk without claiming safety it has not demonstrated.
15. **Correction without erasure.** Material artifacts, measures, methodologies, and rules are versioned; corrections supersede rather than silently rewrite history.

## 7. Structural boundaries

The Venn protocol is not:

- an identity issuer, biometric-enrollment system, global uniqueness index, or token;
- a behavioral-data broker, covert scraper, political-targeting system, or seller of individual opinion profiles;
- a proprietary news authority, even though Venn Reader may be its first reference product;
- a claim that engagement volume equals popular support;
- a claim that verified contributors are representative;
- an autonomous agent permitted to decide what its principal believes;
- a mechanism by which old expression or confirmation becomes a vote;
- a system that gives one company, founder, provider, government, sponsor, or current user majority permanent control; or
- presently a legally recognized public-election system.

## 8. Surfaces and products

The protocol may be implemented through publisher embeds, comment systems, Venn Reader, platform-native controls, share-to-agent flows, browsers, wallets, familiars, MCP or successor agent interfaces, institutional verifiers, calibration tools, and offline command-line verification.

No surface is the protocol.

Carbon Caste may operate Venn Reader and other commercial reference products. They use the same public specifications and receive no privileged data, weighting, conformance, or governance authority. Competing products and publishers have equal technical standing.

## 9. Stewardship and commerce

Carbon Caste Inc is Venn's disclosed founding custodian and commercial reference operator. Its authority is transitional and bounded by the Constitution.

The protocol is intended to pass into independent stewardship before general public collection of person-linked political signals or public conformance of an implementation not operated by Carbon Caste. Carbon Caste may remain a major commercial operator afterward; it may not remain the sole constitutional authority.

Public civic truth is not a proprietary asset. Implementations may charge for infrastructure, hosting, integration, calibration, auditing, support, analysis tools, and availability. They may not sell personal political profiles, exclusive civic results, targeting segments, or preferential influence over the protocol.

## 10. Self-government

Venn shall become its own constitutional laboratory progressively:

1. formation-stage measurement is advisory and founder decisions are explained publicly;
2. verified users and contributors may receive predeclared binding authority over ordinary operating questions;
3. protected amendments use Venn's reference, deliberation, signal, and calibration machinery under constitutional safeguards; and
4. mature governance includes plural users, implementers, operators, auditors, and public-interest representation.

Every governance process states exactly whom it represents. Early users are Venn participants, not humanity.

## 11. Relationship to the Constitution and Roadmap

The Constitution makes this Charter enforceable by defining admissibility, prohibitions, authority, amendment, and enforcement. The Roadmap sequences validation and implementation but cannot amend this Charter.

Where implementation convenience conflicts with the Charter, convenience yields.

Where a new opportunity advances the mission but threatens a durable commitment, it must be redesigned, exceptionally amended, or launched as an explicitly different successor protocol. It may not be smuggled into ordinary product work.
